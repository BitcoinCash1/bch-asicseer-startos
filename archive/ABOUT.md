# ASICSeer — Bitcoin Cash mining pool on StartOS

A Bitcoin Cash mining pool you run yourself.

ASICSeer runs a Bitcoin Cash mining pool on hardware you control, so your miners submit work to your own node instead of a third-party pool that sees every share you find and decides when to pay you. Every block it finds is split in the coinbase between the miners that worked on it, paid straight to their own addresses — you take a fee you set, or none at all. A built-in dashboard shows hashrate, shares and connected workers. Bitcoin Cash Node, Bitcoin Cash Daemon, Flowee the Hub and Knuth are all supported as the node it mines against.
